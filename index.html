Angela Lee

Writer

Angela Lee is a Korean-American college student at University of California, Riverside. Angela is known for her specialization in screenwriting and playwriting, winning two Best Writer awards for high school monologue competitions, Page-to-Stage. Her best known work is in collaboration with R'Shorts: "WALKING".

Angela's love for writing began in high school, where she originally used it as a way to vent about her struggles. Eventually, she found art in the work she was doing and decided to continue pursuing writing, developing her work and continuously improving throughout the years. As she gets older, her appreciation for writing only grows and she hopes to let it continue flourishing. Though she excels at writing, she is an artist in many forms. Angela has an appreciation for music and has acted as a side-job on several occasions. She has a vast amount of history with stage acting and several side-jobs as a screenactor. An artist with many talents, Angela excels at writing. As a college student, Angela continues to balance writing with her school very well. In her collaboration with R'Shorts to bring her film, "WALKING", to life, she works closely with the Director, Director of Photography, and the producers on the release of this short film. Bringing forth responsibility and structure to the making of the film, Angela continues to add a sense of personality to the film. Angela highly appreciates the cast and the crew who have worked with her in this production. She is endlessly grateful for their efforts. Angela thanks her family and friends for their endless support. She thanks her mentors for their advice and support upon aspects of her art and her life

As a freelance writer, she is capable of completing articles, essays, websites, ghostwriting, and what she specializes in the most: screenwriting and playwriting. Angela is able to complete many types of writing with different tones and styles of writing. Though always looking for chances to improve, Angela is open to new opportunities of settling into a writing gig.
